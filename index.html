Who makes this

My wife wouldn't let me drink soju at home.
So I started drinking whisky.

It was 2020. Nobody was going anywhere. Whisky, for reasons I still don't fully understand, passed without comment. So I poured a bourbon, then another, then I started writing about them.

More than 500 expressions later, I still write the same way — including about the bottles I didn't like. I've never taken a sponsored review. Once you start, you can't go back to being believed.

Every glass, hand-checked

The Glencairn Cut Crystal varies from glass to glass. Every box comes to me first, and I hold each glass to the light. The ones that don't pass go back in the box. They don't ship to you.

Why walnut, not oak

Oak gives whisky its colour over years in a barrel. Black walnut already has that colour in the grain, and it darkens with handling instead of wearing out. The centre ring is maple, split by a single line of padauk.

The mark

Most people read the burned-in mark as "enlo". Turn it ninety degrees and it reads 을로 in Korean — the name I've written under for years. Same strokes, two alphabets.
